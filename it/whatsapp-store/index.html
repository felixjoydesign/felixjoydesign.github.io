What is Store.Link?

Store.link allows you to create ecommerce website by using Google Sheets. It's 100% free to create online store. We provide paid plans for power users.

How to create a WhatsApp store using Store.Link?

Creating a WhatsApp store takes just 3 steps! Signup on Store.link, add product data to Google Sheets, and receive orders directly on your WhatsApp number!

Is my WhatsApp ecommerce website Free?

Absolutely! Store.link is free to use, and we do not take any commissions. We offer affordable paid plans for our power users.

Can I create my WhatsApp online store under my domain?

Yes, you can build your WhatsApp ecommerce store on your own domain. Follow our custom domain guide to get started.

How to chat with a customer who placed a WhatsApp order?

Once a customer has placed an order, you will receive a WhatsApp message containing the order details right from your customer's WhatsApp number. You can then continue the chat with your customer on the same chat.

What if my customer doesn't use WhatsApp?

You will still receive all the order details on Google Sheets, and you can follow up with your customer directly.

Is WhatsApp Shop suitable for small businesses?

Yes, a WhatsApp ecommerce store is perfect for small businesses. You get a full online store where customers browse and order, and everything comes straight to your WhatsApp.

What is the difference between a WhatsApp shop and a website store?

With a WhatsApp store, customers place orders on your website and the order details are sent directly to your WhatsApp number. With a regular website store, orders are only received on the website dashboard.
