Is Store.link free?

We have a free forever plan with basic features. You can choose a paid plan if you need powerful features. Visit our pricing page for more details.

How do I create my store?

Just tell our AI what you want to sell, and it will help you create online store in seconds. You can also pick from suggestion chips like Jewelry, Groceries, or Coffee to get started even faster. No templates to choose, no forms to fill.

Is it SEO friendly?

Yes, our ecommerce website builder makes your store fast and search engine friendly. Your store, category, and product pages will perform well in search engines like Google and Bing.

Do you take a revenue cut?

No, we do not take a percentage of your revenue. Store.link is a free ecommerce website builder. There is only a fixed monthly fee for premium features.

Which currencies are supported?

We support almost 180 currencies. So no need to worry, the currency you are planning to use will be in our list. If not, just let us know and we will add it for you.

Does Google Sheet rows sync with my store automatically?

Yes. All changes are instantly reflected in your store. All you need to care about is the product listing in your Google Sheet.

What makes Store.link the best ecommerce website builder?

Store.link is a powerful online store builder that is build on top of Google Sheets. You can manage your products in a spreadsheet and online store updates automatically. No coding needed. Your store is live in minutes. And we don't take commissions from your sale.

Do I need coding knowledge to create an ecommerce website?

To create an ecommerce website using Store.link, you don't need any coding knowledge. You just manage your products in Google Sheets, and Store.link takes care of the rest. Anyone can create an ecommerce website and even set up a WhatsApp store easily with Store.link, no developers needed.

Is Store.link a good ecommerce store builder for small businesses?

Yes! Store.link is built specifically for small businesses. As an ecommerce store builder that runs on Google Sheets, it removes all the technical barriers so you can focus on selling.
