Frequently asked questions.

If you have more questions feel free to contact us.

How does Store.link deliver digital files to customers?

Three ways. Automatic: paste your file's download link in the sheet, and Store.link emails it after payment. Manual: email or WhatsApp the file yourself using the contact captured at checkout. Automated: connect Zapier or Make for custom delivery, CRM updates or license-key flows.

Where are the files actually hosted?

Wherever you already host them: Google Drive, Dropbox, S3, your own site. You paste the share link in the sheet; Store.link delivers it after payment.

Is it really free? What about commissions?

Yes, free forever. Store.link's platform commission is 0% on every sale, every plan. No listing fees, no per-sale cut, no product cap. You only pay your standard payment-processor fee. Paid plans unlock advanced features like a custom domain.

How does Store.link compare to other digital platforms?

Most platforms take 5–10%+ per sale and control customer contact. Store.link's platform commission is 0%, you get direct access to buyer emails, and you can manage your catalog from a Google Sheet instead of one-by-one listing forms.

Can I sell different formats or license tiers of the same product?

Yes. Use sheet variants for PDF / EPUB / MOBI, or Personal / Commercial / Team licenses at different prices.

What payment methods can I accept?

Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Square Payments and custom payment links. Plug in one or several at once.

Do I get my buyers' emails?

Yes. Every order captures the buyer's email at checkout, yours to use for follow-up offers, newsletters and remarketing.

Can I sell AI prompts, Notion templates, ebooks or printables?

Yes, any file you can host. Paste a download link in the sheet, set a price, get paid.

Can I sell online courses?

Yes. Host the videos on YouTube (unlisted), Vimeo or Drive and paste the access link as the product file. For advanced course access, use the webhook to automate it.

Can I use my own domain, currency and language?

Free yourname.store.link subdomain on all plans. Custom domain on any paid plan. Most global currencies supported, and the storefront runs in your buyers' language.

Is the download link unique to each buyer?

Today every buyer receives the same hosted link. For unique buyer-specific links or license keys, use the Zapier / Make webhook to fire off a custom flow.
