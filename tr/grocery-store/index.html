Frequently asked questions.

If you have more questions feel free to contact us.

How do I create an online grocery store?

Sign up on Store.link for free, tell our AI you run a grocery store, and a starter store is created in seconds. Add your grocery items to the connected Google Sheet (name, price, category, unit, image, stock), and your store is live and ready to take orders.

Can I manage hundreds of grocery items?

Yes. Your catalogue lives in Google Sheets, so you can manage hundreds of SKUs the same way you'd manage a spreadsheet. Bulk-paste from a supplier list, bulk-update prices, and toggle stock from a single column. There's no product limit on the free plan.

How do I update grocery prices and stock daily?

Edit the price or stock column in your sheet. The change appears on your storefront instantly. No "publish" step.

Can customers choose a delivery slot or pickup?

Yes. You can create your own delivery methods and time slots (morning, afternoon, evening, next-day). Customers see them at checkout and pick what works.

Do you support Cash on Delivery for grocery orders?

Yes. COD is a built-in payment option. You can also accept online payments via Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay and other regional gateways, or offer both side by side.

Can I receive grocery orders on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every order is sent to your WhatsApp number with full details (items, quantities, customer name, address and delivery slot), so you can reply directly to confirm or clarify.

Is Store.link better than listing on third-party delivery apps?

They serve different purposes. Delivery aggregators help with discovery but charge 20–35% commission and own the customer relationship. Store.link gives you your own grocery website where you keep 100% of every order and have direct contact with every buyer. Many grocers use both: aggregators for new customers, Store.link for repeat buyers.

Can I sell groceries by weight or pack size?

Yes. You can list a product in any unit (kg, g, lb, oz, litre, dozen, packet, piece) and use variant rows in your sheet to offer multiple pack sizes (e.g. 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg) of the same item.

Can I use my own domain like freshmart.com?

Yes. You get a free yourshop.store.link subdomain instantly. Connecting your own domain (freshmart.com, mygrocery.co.uk, etc.) is available on any paid plan.

Will my online grocery store show up on Google?

Yes. Your storefront and each category page support custom meta titles, descriptions and images, so they're set up to be found in search. Fill in clear product names, descriptions and category names for best results.

Does Store.link work in my country and currency?

Yes. Store.link is used by businesses around the world, supports most global currencies, and lets you run the storefront in your customers' language. If your currency isn't already in the list, our support team will add it for you on request.

Is it really free? Any commission?

Yes, free forever. Store.link charges 0% commission on every order on every plan. Optional PRO plans unlock advanced features like a custom domain, but there are never any commissions.
