Frequently asked questions.

If you have more questions feel free to contact us.

What is Store.link?

Store.link is an easy-to-use ecommerce website builder and online store builder built on Google Sheets. You can create online store in minutes by turning your sheet into a modern shop and receive orders via Email, WhatsApp, or Sheets.

How do you charge us?

Store.link can be used under the free plan at no cost. You can upgrade if you need more powerful features like custom domain, advanced analytics etc. The premium plan is on a subscription basis. We charge per store and not per account. You may create multiple stores under one account.

Do you charge transaction fee?

No, we do not take a percentage of your revenue. We won't charge you anything if you are on the free plan, and we will only charge a fixed monthly fee if you are on a paid plan.

How many stores can I create?

You can create multiple stores under the same account. To create more than 3 stores, you need at least one store on our paid plan.

How can I connect with my own domain?

You can change yourstore.store.link to your own custom domain like store.yourcompany.com. You can set the domain from our dashboard and add DNS configuration in your domain.

What would you like to sell?

Tell us about your store and our free ecommerce website builder will create it for you in seconds.
